# Iva: the grocery agent that pays for its own brain

> Most AI agents die the day their owner stops paying the API bill.
> **Iva pays her own bill, out of the money she saves you.**

Iva is a Telegram agent for Indian quick-commerce. Send her a shopping list as text,
a photo of a handwritten list, or a voice note. She checks **Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart and Zepto**
at your location, matches the same product across apps, and tells you the cheapest basket
and how much you save.

Every hunt is paid in **0G**. The payment lands in Iva's own wallet. Iva then **tops up her own
0G Compute account** onchain, keeps a gas reserve, and passes any surplus to her creator.
She reads her own balance before every job and **chooses the best brain she can afford**.

Built at **ETHGlobal Mumbai 2026** for the 0G tracks *Agents That Pay for Themselves* and
*Build with 0G Private Computer*.
